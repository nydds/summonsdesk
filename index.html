FAQ

What people ask first

Are you lawyers?

No. We act as representatives under OATH’s rules — administrative representation only. We say so at the start of every call and in the engagement letter. Where representative registration is required, we complete it first; before that, we prepare everything and the customer signs and files.

Do you guarantee an outcome?

No. We show measured numbers instead. For example, FDNY BF05 cases decided in 2026 were dismissed 1.0% of the time, and when sustained the median penalty was 50% of the ticket. Anyone promising “guaranteed dismissal” is worth avoiding.

It is already in default — is it too late?

A first vacate request within 75 days of the default notice is granted automatically. Between 75 days and one year, you can still apply with reasonable grounds and evidence. After a year, only exceptional circumstances count.

I lost the ticket. Can you still help?

Yes — an address and business name is usually enough to find it in the public data. Photos of the ticket are still the most accurate way to confirm the amount and date.

When do I pay?

After the free review, we confirm the scope and price in writing before starting. Payment by Zelle or cash. Penalties themselves are paid through official channels such as CityPay.

What do you keep about me?

Ticket number, respondent name, address and photos. We never ask for a Social Security number, date of birth or bank account. Photos stay in a case folder and are destroyed after three years; case examples are fully masked.
